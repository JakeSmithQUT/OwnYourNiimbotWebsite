Check the download

Each release has a SHA-256 checksum file. The checksums on this page come from that file. To check a file, calculate its checksum and compare it with the value above.

Windows, in PowerShell:

Get-FileHash .\{%file windows-x64%} -Algorithm SHA256

macOS, in Terminal:

shasum -a 256 ~/Downloads/{%file macos-arm64%}

Linux:

sha256sum ~/Downloads/{%file linux-x64-appimage%}
