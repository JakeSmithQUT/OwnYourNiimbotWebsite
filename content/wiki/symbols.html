The app has 571 symbols in 16 groups. They are line drawings with no grey, so they print cleanly on a thermal printer.

Add a symbol

  1. In the design view, find the Symbols section under the Insert tools.
  2. Click a symbol to add it to the label.
  3. Click All to show every group, or type a word in the search box, for example arrow, fragile or koala. Less shows the common symbols again.

The search finds symbols by name and by group. Symbol names are in English in every language of the app.

Cute animals

The Cute animals group has 126 symbols that were drawn for this app, including a koala, a wombat, a quokka, a kangaroo and a platypus. An animal with a face comes in four expressions: plain, happy, sleepy and in love. Search for cute to see them all.

Change a symbol

Select the symbol to change its position, size and rotation in the right panel. To swap it for a different symbol in the same place, right-click it, point to Replace with, click Symbols and search for the new one.
