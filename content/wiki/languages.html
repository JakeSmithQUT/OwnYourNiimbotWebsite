--- title: Languages description: OwnYourNiimbot is available in 22 languages. Change the language of the app with the globe button or in Settings. section: App and settings order: 2 ---

The app starts in the language of your system if it has that language, else in English. It keeps your choice when you change it.

{%figure languages | The side bar, the Insert panel and the symbols in English, German and Simplified Chinese, side by side.%}

Change the language

  1. Click the globe button at the bottom of the side bar. It shows the current language, for example EN.
  2. Select a language.

You can also change it in Settings, Appearance, Language.

Text that stays in English

Template names, template text and symbol names stay in English in every language. Search for templates and symbols with English words.

Translation corrections

If a translation is wrong or unclear, open an issue on GitHub with the language, the current text and the correction. Native speaker checks for Hindi and Marathi are welcome.
