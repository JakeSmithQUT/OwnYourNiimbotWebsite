--- title: Print history description: The OwnYourNiimbot print history keeps a copy of each label that you print, so you can print it again. section: Printing order: 4 ---

Click History in the side bar. Each print job keeps a copy of the label, so you can print it again even after you change or delete the saved label.

Print a label again

Find the print job in the list and click Print again. The list shows the label, its name and the number of labels in the job.

Clear the history

Click Clear history. The app asks before it deletes the history. You cannot undo this.

Print history location

The history is in the app data folder on this computer, with your labels and settings. See Privacy and the data folder.
