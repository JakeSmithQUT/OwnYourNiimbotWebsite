--- title: Images and photos description: Put a logo or a photo on a NIIMBOT label with OwnYourNiimbot, and convert it to black and white with dithering or a threshold. section: Design labels order: 4 ---

A thermal label printer prints black dots only. When you add an image, the app converts it to black and white. The image settings control how it does that.

Add an image

  1. Click Image in the Insert panel.
  2. Select a PNG, JPEG, GIF, BMP, WebP or SVG file.
  3. Drag a corner to resize the image, then change the settings in the right panel.

The app keeps a copy of the image in the label, so the label still opens if you move or delete the file.

Black and white settings

SettingUse it for
Dither (photos)Photos and images with smooth shading. Grey areas become patterns of dots
Dither, high contrastPhotos that look too dark or grey with the normal dither
Threshold (logos, line art)Logos, icons, text and drawings. Each pixel becomes black or white
Greyscale previewShows the image in grey in the editor. When the label prints, pixels darker than the black level in Settings print black

The sliders change the result:

  • Threshold: with the threshold setting, pixels darker than this value print black.
  • Brightness and Contrast: adjust the image before the conversion.
  • Invert: swaps black and white.

Check the result in the print preview, which shows the label at the printer resolution.

Images from other programs

The printer does not show as a system printer, so other programs cannot print to it directly. To print a design from another program, save it as a PNG image, insert the image on a label, and use the Threshold setting for text and line art.

Label files from other people

An image in a label file must be embedded in the file. The app does not open label files with links to image files or websites, and it rejects images that are too large.

Black level

Settings, Black level sets which grey pixels print black for the whole label. Increase it to make thin or light content print darker. See Settings.
